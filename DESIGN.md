# Candids event album

One shared album after a real gathering. The guest's main job is adding a photo; the host's job is reviewing, sharing and keeping the collection. Photo trading and leaderboards get in the way of that task.

Use a cool photographic palette: cloud #f3f5f9, ink #25304a, cobalt #344cc5, lilac #dce2fa, silver #cbd3df and a small marigold #f0cc72 accent. Georgia gives the Candids wordmark and album title a printed-photo character; Arial keeps controls readable. The gallery, rather than a sales dashboard, occupies most of the page. A quiet right-hand panel holds the host controls on desktop and follows the album on mobile.

The distinctive moment is a stack of original illustrated sample prints, each with a photographic caption. They are explicitly fictional artwork, not customer photographs. Avoid fake upload counts, reviews, attendees or results. Only the demo has sample counts; a real event starts empty.

The design was revised from a generic grid of dashboard cards to a photo-led contact sheet with an album heading and one functional control panel. Use borders to show image edges, not to put every paragraph in a box. Guest and host controls have different permissions in the API as well as different presentation.

The first desktop screenshot exposed the handwritten hero note overlapping a print caption; the redundant decorative note was removed. Desktop and 390 px mobile screenshots were reviewed for readable captions, clear fictional-demo labelling, working controls and overflow. On mobile the upload panel precedes the gallery, while host controls follow it. Guest mode hides host controls and limits the collection until sharing; blocking is reflected in the sample view as well.
